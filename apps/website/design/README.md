Design ONE exceptionally refined original brand symbol for Wot CI, a developer tool for continuous integration and mini-program delivery. Asset: standalone logo symbol, transparent background, no text, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no surrounding tile. Art direction: Swiss modernist geometric identity meets elegantly folded paper ribbon. A compact asymmetric loop formed by two interlocking broad ribbons, suggesting continuous circulation merging into one forward/upward movement. The outer silhouette should subtly evoke an open C, with a deliberate crisp diagonal cut at upper right; a small integrated blade subtly suggests I without spelling letters. Strong iconic silhouette and beautiful deliberate negative space. Balanced tension between broad rounded outer curves and sharp diagonal terminals. Use exactly two solid ink colors: deep forest green #294C3D and muted sage green #91B49A, transparent negative space. Flat vector-like solid shapes, no outlines, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no sparkles, no gears, no checkmark, no infinity clipart, no literal CI typography. At most 3 simple connected forms, aesthetically sophisticated and optically balanced at 32px favicon size. Center symbol with generous but not excessive margin; symbol occupies 80 percent of square canvas. This must feel like a custom designed premium engineering brand identity, not a stock UI icon.
